Kalix by Artiros

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

  • Kalix collects no data about you.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your calculations, graphs, lists and notes stay on your device.
  • Purchases go through Apple. Artiros never sees your payment details.

What Kalix does with your data

Kalix is a scientific graphing calculator for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with an Apple Watch app. Everything you create in it, such as calculations and their history, variables and functions, graphs and 3D graphs, lists, finance worksheets and notes, is saved in the app's own storage on your device. Your settings (angle mode, display digits, graph and keypad options) are saved on your device too.

Kalix has no server of its own and does not send this information to Artiros or to anyone else. Calculating and graphing work without an internet connection.

To convert currencies, the Convert page downloads the daily euro reference exchange rates that the European Central Bank publishes as a public file. That request carries nothing about you or your work; like any web request, it reaches the European Central Bank's servers from your device's internet address.

What Kalix does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools, and no third-party code.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no sharing or selling of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. Kalix asks for no permissions.

Purchases

Kalix offers a free 7-day trial and a one-time Unlock, both sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account. Artiros never receives your name, email address or payment details.

To know whether Kalix is unlocked, the app checks on your device which Kalix purchases your Apple Account owns (the date the trial started and whether Unlock was bought). This check stays on your device. How Apple handles purchases is described in Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Backups and diagnostics from Apple

Like other apps, Kalix's data can be included in the backups your device makes for you, such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer. Those backups belong to you and are managed by Apple's services, not by Artiros.

If you turned on sharing with app developers in your device's privacy settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with developers. That comes from Apple under your settings; Kalix itself contains no code that collects them.

Export your data

You can save a copy of all your data at any time: open Settings in Kalix, go to the Kalix section, choose Export My Data, then Export as Text or Export All Data (JSON), and pick where to save the file. After the free trial, the same export stays available from View and Export My Data, even if you do not unlock Kalix.

Delete your data

  • Inside the app, you can delete calculations, past sessions, variables and other items one by one, or delete all past calculations from History.
  • To delete everything on iPhone or iPad, delete the app: touch and hold the Kalix icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Its data is deleted with it.
  • On a Mac, move Kalix from Applications to the Trash. Its data folder, the Kalix folder in ~/Library/Containers, can then be moved to the Trash too.
  • Copies in your own backups or exported files are under your control and are removed when you delete them.

Deleting the app does not cancel or remove your purchases. They stay with your Apple Account and come back with Restore Purchases.

Children

Kalix is rated 4+ and is suitable for students of all ages. Because Kalix collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If Kalix's handling of data ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the app is released, with a new date at the top. The privacy details on the App Store will be updated at the same time.
