What Kalix is

Kalix is a scientific graphing calculator with a computer algebra system. It gives exact answers (fractions, roots, symbolic results), solves and simplifies equations, works with derivatives, integrals and limits, and draws graphs in 2D and 3D. It also has a Geometry page, Lists & Spreadsheet, Data & Statistics, Notes with live math, Programs, and an equation Workbench that solves step by step.

Kalix runs on iPhone with iOS 27 or later, iPad with iPadOS 27 or later, and Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 27 or later.

The free trial and the one-time Unlock

  • Free to download. The first time you open Kalix, a screen explains the trial and shows the price of Unlock.
  • 7-day free trial. Tap Start Free Trial to use every feature for 7 days. Nothing is charged, and the trial never turns into a payment by itself.
  • After 7 days. Calculating, graphing and editing stop until you unlock Kalix. You can still view and export your calculations, variables and graphs.
  • Unlock once. Unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. You can buy it at any time: on the first screen, during the trial in Settings, or after the trial.
  • iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. One purchase unlocks Kalix on all your devices signed in with the same Apple Account. On Apple Watch, start the trial, unlock or restore in the Kalix app on your iPhone; the watch follows.

The trial is tied to your Apple Account: it starts once, and deleting and reinstalling the app does not start a new one.

Restore Purchases

If Kalix asks you to start the trial or to unlock again on a new device, or after reinstalling, restore your purchase:

  1. Make sure the device is connected to the internet and signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy Unlock.
  2. Open Kalix and tap Restore Purchases. It is on the first screen, on the screen shown after the trial, and in Settings, in the Kalix section.
  3. Kalix unlocks as soon as the App Store confirms the purchase. In Settings, the Kalix section then shows the status Unlocked.

Where Settings is

  • iPhone: tap the menu button at the top left of any page, then Settings.
  • iPad: Settings in the sidebar.
  • Mac: Kalix menu > Settings, or Command comma.

If Kalix says no earlier purchase was found, check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account. You can see your purchases in the App Store under your account.

Export your data

Your work stays on your device. To save a copy:

  1. Open Settings in Kalix and go to the Kalix section.
  2. Choose Export My Data.
  3. Choose Export as Text for a readable file (calculations, variables, functions and graphs), or Export All Data (JSON) for everything Kalix saves, including 3D graphs, geometry, lists, notes and programs.
  4. Pick where to save the file, for example in Files on iPhone and iPad or in a folder on your Mac.

After the free trial, if you do not unlock Kalix, tap View and Export My Data on the screen shown after the trial. You can read your data there and export it the same way.

Questions

Is Kalix a subscription?

No. The trial is free and Unlock is a single payment. There is nothing to cancel.

Will I be charged when the trial ends?

No. Kalix only charges you if you tap Unlock and confirm the purchase with Apple.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to start the trial, buy Unlock or restore a purchase, and to refresh the exchange rates used for currency conversion. Once the trial or Unlock is on your device, everything else in Kalix works offline.

Do I need an account?

No. Kalix has no account and no sign-in. Purchases use your Apple Account through the App Store.

Does my work sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

No. Each device keeps its own work, on the device. The purchase is shared across your devices, but your calculations are not. Use Export My Data to keep a copy.

What happens to my data when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your data stays on your device, and you can view and export it at any time. When you unlock Kalix, you continue where you left off.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made with Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I switch between radians and degrees?

In Settings, under Calculation: Angle. The same section sets exact or decimal answers, the number of digits shown and complex numbers.

What data does Kalix collect?

None. See the privacy policy.
